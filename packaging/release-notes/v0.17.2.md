## Highlights

- **Native YouTube Music playback and search.** Stream YouTube Music tracks, albums, artists, and playlists without an embedded browser engine, powered by `yt-dlp` and `ffmpeg`.
- **Offline downloads and local library.** Save tracks and collections locally with SQLite indexing, playlist management, and queue controls.
- **Equalizer, MilkDrop visualizer, and Winamp mini player.** Built-in multi-band EQ, MilkDrop visual presets, custom built-in Resonix Winamp skins, and synced lyrics.
- **Desktop integration and auto-updates.** MPRIS media controls, tray minimization, Discord Rich Presence, and Ed25519-signed GitHub Release updates.
